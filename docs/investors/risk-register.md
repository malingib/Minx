# Minx — Initial Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation / control | Escalation trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Slower user acquisition | Medium | High | Cohort acquisition, channel attribution and retention gates | MAU or activation below 70% of approved quarterly plan |
| Weak buyer conversion | Medium | High | Category focus, creator-led merchandising and checkout tests | Buyer conversion below 80% of plan for two consecutive months |
| High media delivery cost | Medium | High | Adaptive bitrate, usage caps, stream architecture and cost alerts | Media cost per viewer-hour above 125% of model |
| Payment settlement delays | Medium | High | Provider SLAs, reconciliation and liquidity buffer | Settlement ageing exceeds contractual provider terms |
| Fraud, disputes or refunds | Medium | High | Risk rules, monitoring, refund and dispute workflows | Loss/dispute rate exceeds board-approved threshold |
| Regulatory uncertainty | Medium | High | Kenyan counsel review, licensed partners and no customer stored-value wallet at launch | Counsel identifies a licence or approval requirement |
| Trust and safety incident | Medium | High | Reporting, moderation, evidence, appeals and escalation rota | Severe incident or response SLA breach |
| Security/data breach | Low/Medium | Very High | Least privilege, secrets management, testing, logging and response plan | Confirmed unauthorised access or data exposure |
| Delivery/quality slippage | Medium | High | Milestone SOWs, CI gates, independent QA and rollback | Critical-path milestone more than two weeks late |
| Vendor dependency | Medium | Medium | Provider-neutral media control plane and documented exit paths | Material price/service change or sustained outage |
| Funding shortfall | Medium | High | Tranche gates, monthly cash forecast and contingency controls | Forecast runway below six months |
