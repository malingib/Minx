# Minx — Investor Structure and Governance Framework

## Funding approach
Use a milestone-based process and avoid fixing valuation or dilution before financial, corporate, legal and product diligence. A lead investor or small aligned syndicate is preferable to a fragmented cap table.

## Potential instruments
| Instrument | Appropriate context | Negotiation items |
|---|---|---|
| Priced equity | Lead investor ready to price after diligence | Pre-money valuation, dilution, board/observer rights, reserved matters, information rights, pro-rata rights, founder retention and exit provisions |
| Convertible instrument | Valuation premature, but capital needed for defined milestones | Conversion trigger, discount/cap if any, maturity, interest if any, qualified financing threshold, repayment exposure and investor rights |
| Milestone tranche / SAFE-style structure | Staged capital and deferred valuation are desired | Kenyan legal suitability, tranche gates, conversion mechanics, long-stop dates and protections |
| Strategic commercial partnership | Partner contributes distribution, creator/merchant supply or infrastructure | Exclusivity, data/IP ownership, service levels, revenue share, termination, conflict controls and governance |

No valuation, equity percentage, guaranteed return, dividend, redemption promise or exit date is proposed here. All instruments require qualified Kenyan legal and tax review.

## Suggested release gates
1. **Build foundation:** architecture, signed SOWs, IP assignment, company-controlled repo/cloud ownership, CI and security plan.
2. **Feature-complete beta:** critical user journeys, payment sandbox, verified webhooks, reconciliation tests and no critical security defects.
3. **Controlled beta:** cohort KPIs, reliability, trust/safety, support and spend reporting.
4. **Commercial launch:** compliance and app-release readiness, creator/merchant supply and support capacity.
5. **Scale extension:** repeat usage, monetisation, CAC/payback and contribution margin evidence.

## Governance principles
- Board-approved budget, monthly management accounts and quarterly investor reporting.
- Narrow reserved matters for material share issuance, borrowing, sale of core IP/company, related-party transactions, major budget deviations and winding-up.
- No day-to-day product vetoes or unrestricted access to personal data.
- Company-controlled IP, source, domains, cloud accounts, signing keys and provider relationships.
- Founder remuneration and roles documented; contractor and founder IP assignments executed.
- Privacy-safe aggregated investor reporting; investor rights do not confer access to customer personal data.

## Before agreeing terms
Validate the fully diluted cap table, corporate records, IP ownership, tax position, legal/regulatory perimeter, monthly cash forecast, provider quotations, downside case and governance requirements. Engage Kenyan counsel for term sheets and transaction documents.
