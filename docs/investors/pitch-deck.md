# Minx — Investor Pitch Deck Narrative

## 1. Minx
**Social. Live. Market.** An African-first creator, community and commerce platform.

## 2. The opportunity
Creators build audiences but monetise across disconnected tools. Merchants need trusted creator-led discovery that converts into sales. Consumers need local content, familiar payments and low-friction checkout. Kenya is the initial test market; market sizing remains a diligence item.

## 3. One connected experience
Short video, social posts, livestreams, messaging, communities, creator monetisation and merchant commerce in one mobile-first product.

## 4. The signature journey
1. Watch a creator livestream.
2. Explore a pinned product and variants.
3. Check out with M-Pesa.
4. Receive order confirmation.
5. Seller fulfils and buyer tracks the order.

## 5. Why Minx
Commerce-native live video, local-first payments and connectivity, unified creator/merchant tools, auditable financial controls and a modular architecture. These hypotheses require pilot validation.

## 6. Product and architecture
React Native + Expo mobile apps; Go modular Core; separate Finance service and database; managed PostgreSQL; NATS JetStream; LiveKit interactive RTC; Cloudflare Stream broadcast/VOD; managed AWS and Cloudflare. Production region, vendor pricing and performance gates remain open pending evidence.

## 7. Go-to-market
Recruit anchor creators and merchants in selected categories; run controlled cohorts; measure activation, retention, watch time, buyer conversion, fulfilment and trust/safety; expand only when contribution margin and support capacity justify it.

## 8. Controlled beta targets
- 10,000 registered users.
- 2,500 monthly active users.
- 2,500 video-viewing hours per month.
- 150 peak concurrent livestream viewers.
These are targets, not validated outcomes or capacity guarantees.

## 9. Revenue model
Marketplace commission, Minx share of creator monetisation, merchant subscriptions and advertising. Costs include payment processing, media, staff, marketing, infrastructure, support/moderation and G&A.

## 11. Funding bridge
Preliminary planning envelope: **KES 29.70M**, comprising KES 2.45M build/beta, KES 5M commercial launch, peak cumulative operating deficit and 20% working-capital reserve. This is not a firm raise amount until the monthly model is validated.

## 12. Milestones and risk
Build foundation → feature-complete beta → controlled beta → commercial launch → scale extension. Key risks: acquisition, retention, monetisation, media cost, payment/regulatory scope, trust and safety, security and runway.

## 13. The investment conversation
Seeking aligned capital and strategic support. Financing instrument, valuation, dilution, governance and investor rights to be agreed after diligence and Kenyan legal/tax review. No guaranteed investor return or exit is represented.
